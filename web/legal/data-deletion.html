Deleting your data

Last updated 21 September 2026

You can ask for your Zenoeats account and the personal information held with it to be deleted. This page explains how, and what happens to your past orders.

How to ask

Email PLACEHOLDER@example.com from the address you use to sign in, with the subject Delete my data.

Sending it from the address on the account is what lets us confirm the request is yours. If you cannot, say so in the message and we will ask you to confirm another way — we will not delete an account on the word of an address we cannot connect to it.

You will get confirmation within PLACEHOLDER days, and the deletion will be complete within PLACEHOLDER days of that.

If you signed in with Google, Apple or Facebook

Removing Zenoeats from your Google, Apple or Facebook account stops it being used to sign in, but it does not by itself delete what Zenoeats holds. Email us as above to have that removed.

What gets deleted

  • Your sign-in details, which are held by our identity provider.
  • Your name, email address, phone number and saved address on your Zenoeats profile.
  • Your saved favourite items.
  • Your ability to sign in. The account is closed.

What is kept, and why

Orders you have already placed and paid for are kept. They are the restaurant’s business and tax record of a sale that happened, and both the restaurant and Zenoeats are required to be able to account for it.

A kept order still carries the name, phone number and address that were on it when it was placed, because that is what the record of the sale consists of. It is no longer linked to a usable account.

The payment itself is held by Stripe on the restaurant’s account and is subject to the restaurant’s and Stripe’s own retention rules.

If you ordered as a guest

There is no account to delete. A guest record that never became an order is removed automatically after about 45 days. If a guest order was placed, it is kept as described above; email us if you want the contact details on it looked at.

Deleting one order

An individual paid order cannot be deleted on request, for the reason above. If something on it is wrong — a misspelled name, the wrong phone number — email us and it can be corrected.
